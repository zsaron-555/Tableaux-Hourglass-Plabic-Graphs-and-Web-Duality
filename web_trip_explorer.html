How the values are computed

For boundary b, promote Pb−1(T). If aᵢ crosses from row i+1 to row i, then promᵢ(b) is aᵢ+b−1 modulo 16, with residues labeled 1–16. The colored arrows show the entries sliding upward across the three row boundaries.

Trips take the i-th left at white vertices and the i-th right at black vertices. Hourglass edges use the paper’s half-twist, reconstructed from cyclic order and multiplicity.

Reference paper · §3.1, Definition 3.4; Definitions 4.6 and 4.12. A matching trip tuple alone does not prove full reducedness or a tensor identity.
